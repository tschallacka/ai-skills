# www

**A brake the human can pull, and one the agent pulls on itself when it is
thrashing.**

Two triggers share one behaviour: the human typing `www`, or the agent
noticing it is retrying variants of a failing command, re-reading the same
files, or guessing at a cause it has not measured. Either one means stop -
no further tool calls, no continuing the current line of attack.

The brake only lifts once three questions are answered, in order and in
their exact wording: what do we have, what are the values, what are we
trying to achieve. Each forces something a thrashing agent has usually lost
- the measured facts instead of an impression, the concrete particulars
instead of the abstract shape of the problem, and the goal instead of the
symptom being chased. Only then may work continue, and only as one reasoned
step or a numbered question - never another speculative attempt.

Read `SKILL.md` for the exact wording and the gate.
